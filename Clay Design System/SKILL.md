## Clay essentials
- **Canvas is cream** (`--color-canvas` #fffaf0), never cool gray — including the footer (never dark).
- **Display type** = Plain Black 500 + negative tracking (substituted with Inter 500); **body** = Inter. Never mix the two roles.
- **Saturated feature cards** (pink → teal → lavender → peach → ochre → cream) are the signature element; cycle them, never repeat back-to-back.
- **Primary CTA** is near-black, 12px radius, sentence case.
- **3D claymation illustrations** are the brand's voltage — they are commissioned assets, not tokens.
